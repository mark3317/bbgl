[Русская версия / Russian release notes](https://github.com/mark3317/bbgl/blob/main/docs/releases/1.1.0.ru.md)

BBGL 1.1.0 includes BBSC-GL 1.1.0, with improvements to the launcher, connections, respawning and co-op transitions.

### Launcher

- Updated the home screen layout, typography and graphics preset selector.
- Improved launch cancellation and the firewall setup dialog.
- Added shader preparation progress in the game window.
- Fixed mouse recapture after restoring the window or regaining focus.
- Improved network handshakes and handling of delayed messages from previous connections.

### Co-op

- Joining a host on another map now uses a single load. Removed extra connection screens and legacy loading overlays.
- Restored smooth fades for co-op respawns without reloading the map, both in the open world and when respawning from spectator mode after a boss victory. Notifications appear after the fade finishes.
- Improved quitting through the in-game menu, movement restrictions during exit, and protection against an unnecessary boss reload.
- Refined repeat summons, stale session cleanup and chalice dungeon transitions.
- Updated the original bells and their English/Russian prompts; prevented duplicate grants of co-op items.
- Improved PvP kill tracking, Blood Dreg rewards and local Vileblood ranking.
- Reduced background work for progress synchronization and enemy scaling; optimized quest event lookup.
- Added shared Hunter's Dream ending logic and transition to the host's next NG cycle, with a journal for recovering an interrupted finale.

### Validation and known limitations

The respawn fade passed a C++ fixture test and the DLL was built, but this latest change has not yet been validated in gameplay. Shared endings, NG+ and recovery after interruption also require gameplay confirmation. An intermittent connection failure before joining a room remains under investigation. Coverage of larger parties and rare chalice/progression scenarios is ongoing; these issues are not claimed to be resolved.

The release package passed build, ZIP integrity and payload hygiene checks. The packaged launcher UI, shadNet startup and account registration/login passed smoke tests.
